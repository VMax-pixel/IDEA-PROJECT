Why it's different

It starts where you are. You don't need a perfect prompt. You start with a feeling or a rough thought.

It guides you. Instead of an open chat box, your guide asks one easy question at a time and builds on your answers.

It shows, not just tells. You get palettes and images to react to, so you can say "more like this" instead of searching for words.

Your ideas stay organised. Save them and come back to keep shaping.
